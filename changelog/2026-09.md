# September 2026

## 0.3.1 — 2026-09-03

Reworked [`book-reference-extractor`](../skills/writing/book-reference-extractor/SKILL.md) around a confirmed reader interview and long-form prose.

### Changed behavior

- The extractor now builds its author map privately, then runs a grilling-style reader interview even when supplied documents contain no reader notes.
- Final drafting is gated on a shared-understanding checkpoint that the reader confirms or corrects.
- The default output is continuous, memo-length prose rather than the fixed proposition/glossary/observations/rules template.
- Author vocabulary and meaningful supplied phrases are preserved as source language; reader wording is preserved for judgments and experience. The workflow still avoids imitation of a named author's distinctive voice.
- Added sentence-level provenance and source-and-voice audits for author claims, reader judgments, extractor inferences, and possible applications.
- Updated the Writing router so the new long-form, interview-gated behavior is discoverable from book highlights, excerpts, and notes.

### Evaluation

- Added cases covering absent reader notes, rough notes as hypotheses, the interview gate, and long-form source-faithful output in [`evals/writing-workflows.md`](../evals/writing-workflows.md).

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-02

Added the trial [`Business`](../skills/business/SKILL.md) category and four portable workflows adapted from the owner’s legacy business prompts and selected methods from [gstack](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack):

- [`business-idea-review`](../skills/business/business-idea-review/SKILL.md): evaluate business ideas and strategic bets.
- [`customer-value-proposition`](../skills/business/customer-value-proposition/SKILL.md): frame and test customer value.
- [`decision-navigator`](../skills/business/decision-navigator/SKILL.md): guide consequential business decisions.
- [`business-problem-solving`](../skills/business/business-problem-solving/SKILL.md): structure complex business problems and analysis plans.

### Changed behavior

- Business work now has a dedicated router that can reference existing coding, miscellaneous, and writing skills without copying them.
- The workflows require fact/inference/assumption separation, explicit falsifiers, India-specific context where relevant, and downside protection.
- Fixed numerical gates such as universal LTV/CAC thresholds, moat counts, and 10x claims are treated as prompts rather than proof.
- Private business-context documents remain external to the public skill library.

### Provenance and evaluation

- Selected gstack methods are pinned to commit `0d1bd5616c0ef096bb7ccee336f63c60ee408618`, version `1.79.0`, MIT license, and recorded in [`registry.yaml`](../registry.yaml).
- Adaptations remove gstack-specific runtime paths, telemetry, private memory, host integrations, promotional text, and engineering-only orchestration.
- Behavioral cases are recorded in [`evals/business-workflows.md`](../evals/business-workflows.md).

### Added: owner-defined workflow skills

- [goldilocks-review](../skills/coding/goldilocks-review/SKILL.md): gate non-trivial work after the PRD/spec and before ticket creation. It prioritizes minimal coupling and operational simplicity, and prefers deep modules behind limited interfaces.

### Added: Thermos major-change review

- [thermos](../skills/coding/thermos/thermos/SKILL.md): orchestrate both Thermos review passes for a major change.
- [thermo-nuclear-review](../skills/coding/thermos/thermo-nuclear-review/SKILL.md): inspect correctness, regressions, security, developer experience, and feature-gate leaks.

The required major-change sequence is `thermos → fix material findings → code-review`.

- [`ask-matt`](../skills/coding/matt/engineering/ask-matt/SKILL.md) now routes multi-session work through `goldilocks-review` between `to-spec` and `to-tickets`.
- [`to-tickets`](../skills/coding/matt/engineering/to-tickets/SKILL.md) now requires an approved `docs/decisions/<feature-slug>-goldilocks.md` before drafting or publishing tickets.
- [`implement`](../skills/coding/matt/engineering/implement/SKILL.md) now requires Thermos before Matt’s final `code-review` for major changes.
- Selected pstack skills no longer depend on Cursor model slugs, private Cursor paths, or `agents/openai.yaml` metadata. They use the current harness and project layout.
- The pstack verification workflow now creates project-local verification skills under `.agents/skills/`.
- Canonical skills remain portable. Claude Code, Codex, and Pi invocation and discovery details live in [`adapters/`](../adapters/).
- Continual learning is proposal-first. It does not edit `AGENTS.md` or `CLAUDE.md` without approval.
