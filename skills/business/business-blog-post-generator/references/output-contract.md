# Blog post output contract

Return these sections in order unless the owner requests a different format.

## Gap diagnosis

State the chosen gap, reader decision, business relevance, evidence, confidence, and the main alternatives rejected.

## Confirmed content brief

Record the owner's confirmed:

- reader and situation;
- business objective;
- reader promise and central claim;
- article mode and outline;
- evidence, examples, numbers, and attribution;
- service, industry, geography, and CTA;
- exclusions and unresolved risks.

## Article

Provide:

- title;
- optional deck or standfirst;
- suggested slug;
- meta title and meta description when search is in scope;
- article body with descriptive sentence-case headings;
- CTA that matches the confirmed business objective.

## Verification note

List:

- claims verified from the site or owner-supplied sources;
- claims labelled as estimates, anecdotes, or inferences;
- contradictions not used as fact;
- unsupported claims removed;
- external sources still needed;
- approval or fact checks still required before publication.
